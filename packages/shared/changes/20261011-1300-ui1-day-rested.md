# UI-1: day-by-day play, `day_rested` (additive)

Date: 2026-10-11 13:00 IST. Lane: UI-1, with BE-2 review. Migration `20261011130000_daily_life_events.sql`.

## `packages/shared` (additive)

- New `SyncEvent` type `day_rested`: `{ month, week, day, health, sanity }`, sent each night the
  student rests at home (`day` is the day that ended; health and sanity are the clamped changes).
  `week_slept` is still sent when a week ends.
- `ClassWellbeingRow` gains `rest_days`.

Nothing was renamed or removed.

## Backend

- `shop_purchase`, `work_shift` and `school_attended` also keep `day` (1–7). Older clients that send
  no day are still accepted.
- `class_wellbeing` counts `work_shifts` and `school_days` per day (per week for older events with no
  day) and gains `rest_days`. It was dropped and recreated, and its grants were set again
  (facilitators only).
